## [Fil-C Tackles Memory Safety in Context Switching](https://fil-c.org/context_switches) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Fil-C introduces memory-safe alternatives to traditional context switching mechanisms like setjmp/longjmp and ucontext, preventing invalid stack frame access. This addresses a long-standing vulnerability in systems programming, where unsafe context switching can lead to crashes or security exploits. It could make C-based systems more reliable and secure. Fil-C ensures that longjmp only works when called from a stack frame that is a descendant of the frame that created the jmp_buf, preventing use-after-free of stack frames. The approach also handles register spilling and allocation complexities.

hackernews · modeless · Jun 30, 00:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48727177)

**Background**: Context switching mechanisms like setjmp/longjmp and ucontext are widely used in C for non-local jumps and coroutines. However, they are inherently unsafe because they can jump to invalid stack frames, leading to undefined behavior. Fil-C is a research project aiming to retrofit memory safety onto C programs.
