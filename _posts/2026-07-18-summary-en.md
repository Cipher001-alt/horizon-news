## [Zilog Z80 Celebrates 50th Anniversary](https://goliath32.com/blog/z80.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Zilog Z80 microprocessor, first released in 1976, has reached its 50th anniversary, marked by a blog post and community discussion reflecting on its legacy.
