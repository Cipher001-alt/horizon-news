## [AI Makes Programming Differently Difficult](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/ai-didnt-make-programming-easier-it-just-made-it-differently-difficult/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An opinion article argues that AI does not make programming easier but shifts the difficulty from recalling syntax and algorithms to evaluating the correctness and architectural soundness of AI-generated code. This insight reframes the impact of AI on software development, highlighting that developers now need stronger judgment and architectural skills rather than just coding fluency, which could reshape education and hiring practices. The article emphasizes that the hard part moves from recall ('How do I write this?') to judgment ('Does this actually make sense?'), and that code remains the concrete truth being executed, while prompts and plans are temporary artifacts.

hackernews · tchalla · Jul 21, 18:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48996197)

**Background**: Traditionally, programming difficulty lay in remembering syntax, algorithms, and debugging low-level errors. With AI code generation, developers spend more time reviewing, testing, and validating AI output, which requires deep understanding of system design and correctness.

**Discussion**: Commenters largely agree with the article, noting that experience is crucial for evaluating AI-generated code, and that the shift mirrors challenges in AI-assisted writing. Some argue AI merely reveals the inherent difficulty of architectural judgment, and that future AI may assist with that too.
